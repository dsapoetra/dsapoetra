I live in Bintaro, South Tangerang, and I lead the Payments and Monetization
team at OLX Indonesia: eleven engineers, seventeen backend services, and a PHP
monolith we are retiring piece by piece. I studied at ITB and have spent a
little over ten years writing backend systems in Go, Node.js and Java, mostly
in fintech and marketplaces. I still read code before it ships, because I think
a manager who cannot do that eventually stops knowing what the team is carrying.

The writing is not a side project so much as the same habit pointed elsewhere.
Payments teach you to care about what is missing: the transaction that did not
reconcile, the line that does not agree. Writing starts in the same place, and
these days it goes on Medium. I run early in the morning, and I am forty-seven
books into a 125-book reading plan.
