title: Ledger
date: "2026-09-06"
lang: en
---

At the end of the day the numbers must agree,
and mostly they do, which is its own small mercy.
What does not agree I carry home on the train,
a sum that will not settle, a name without a face,
and I set it on the table next to the rice
and let it sit there until it becomes a poem.
